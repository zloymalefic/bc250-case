# Flush rear service system v0.1

The red Nyacom-style rear insert remains common to all PSU and I/O variants:

- rear panel: 125 × 165 × 4 mm;
- two identical standard module openings: 104.6 × 48.6 mm;
- upper module: BC-250 I/O cassette;
- lower module: Cisco or FlexATX interface cassette;
- main panel and both cassettes use concealed hooks;
- no PSU carrier or adapter protrudes beyond the rear panel;
- release notches are placed between modules rather than on visible outer edges.

Current outputs deliberately include an I/O blank, a provisional ventilated Cisco insert, and a provisional FlexATX envelope insert. Connector apertures, screw holes, IEC inlet, PSU latch access, and cable strain relief cannot be finalized without physical measurements.

Because both openings share one standard interface, later measurement changes require reprinting only a 104 × 48 mm insert rather than the red rear panel or chassis.
