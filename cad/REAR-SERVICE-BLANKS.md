# Vertical rear service blanks v0.1

Both rear-cover variants now share the same two vertical magnetic openings:

- board/I/O blank: 71.30 × 144.30 × 3.00 mm;
- PSU blank: 47.30 × 144.30 × 3.00 mm;
- nominal perimeter clearance: 0.35 mm per edge;
- four pairs of 8 × 2 mm magnets per blank;
- 2 mm structural shoulder behind each opening.

The shallow horizontal rear cover and the tapered tower base contain identical
seats and receivers. The blanks can therefore be transferred between either
case orientation without reprinting them.

The current parts are intentionally solid. Connector cut-outs belong to these
small replaceable blanks, not to either structural rear cover. This allows the
BC-250 I/O arrangement or PSU adapter to change without reprinting the case or
tower base.

Magnet pockets are 8.25 mm diameter and 2.20 mm deep. Print a pocket coupon
before a full cover. The structural horizontal/vertical rear cover itself stays
on M3 fasteners because the tower version carries the enclosure weight; only
its connector blanks are magnetic.
