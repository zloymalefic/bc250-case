# BC-250 board tray v0.1

The tray is a structural perimeter frame rather than a solid plate:

- overall tray: 318 × 146.2 × 4 mm;
- BC-250 proxy: 311.7 × 144 mm;
- nominal side clearance: 1.1 mm per side;
- board plane: Z=16 mm;
- continuous open backplate channel: 12 mm above the tray base;
- eight elongated chassis slots permit 16 mm longitudinal adjustment;
- four generic edge-support stations;
- four M4 anchors provide a separate load path for future JF13K radiator transport supports;
- centre remains open for backplate airflow and inspection.

The tray is divided at X=159 mm:

- front print length including tongues: 167 mm;
- rear print length: 159 mm;
- two low-profile tongues align the perimeter rails without crossing the airflow opening.

The current edge supports are placeholders, not confirmed BC-250 mounting points. Their final position and clamp geometry require the physical board or a trusted STEP model with verified mounting-hole coordinates. Likewise, the JF13K anchors are structural interface points; the adjustable towers above them are deferred until the installed cooler envelope is measured.
