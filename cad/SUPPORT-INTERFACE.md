# Common support interface v0.1

The common chassis remains identical in horizontal and vertical use. Orientation is selected by replacing only the external support set.

## Fasteners

- four M4 countersunk screws;
- 4.5 mm printed clearance holes;
- 8.5 mm maximum countersunk head diameter;
- threaded inserts or captive nuts belong to the chassis-side interface and are not yet frozen;
- all coordinates remain provisional until the final chassis wall construction is selected.

## Horizontal set

Two identical 142 × 28 × 8 mm transverse skids. Each skid has two chassis screws on the common 114 mm pitch and two 12 × 1.5 mm recesses for optional rubber pads. The parts fit even a small printer and can be replaced independently.

## Vertical set

Two identical 230 × 34 × 23 mm transverse feet. A 171 mm cradle accepts the 170 mm short end with 0.5 mm nominal clearance per side. Each foot extends 29.5 mm beyond each side of the chassis, giving a 230 mm anti-tip footprint without creating one oversized printed base.

## Status

These are functional prototype parts, not final production geometry. Before printing, verify the actual wall thickness, insert type, screw-head dimensions, and whether the standing end needs additional retention against lifting.
