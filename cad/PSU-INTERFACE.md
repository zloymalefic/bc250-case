# Fully internal universal PSU interface v0.1

The NexGen reference contributes the principle of one chassis accepting separate PSU mounts. The external/protruding implementation is intentionally not copied.

- common perforated internal rail: 252 × 104 × 6 mm;
- rail ends at X=295 mm;
- rear inner plane is X=318 mm;
- nominal internal service margin: 23 mm;
- separate Cisco and FlexATX adapter geometries;
- only a flush rear connector/exhaust panel is visible from outside;
- all equipment dimensions remain provisional until measured.

The Cisco adapter currently uses side guides and an internal end stop. Its actual latch receiver cannot be finished until the UCSC-PSU-650W V02 latch dimensions are available.
